Privacy

Self-Healing Data Pipeline Platform · last updated 16 October 2026

In short: this is a portfolio demo. When you sign in with Google, it keeps only your Google account id, email address and name, to know who you are and to keep your test runs separate from everyone else's. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to track you.

What is stored

  • Your Google account: account id, email address, name, and when you first and last signed in. No password is ever seen or stored: Google handles the sign-in.
  • Your test runs: the incidents, audit steps, held-back rows and escalations created when you run a test or upload a file. Only you (and the site owner, as admin) can see them.
  • One login cookie that keeps you signed in for 7 days. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Short-lived counters that limit how many runs you can start per minute.

What is shared

  • Google Gemini: if a test file is broken, its error text (and, for broken JSON, the file's contents) is sent to Google Gemini so the agents can analyse or repair it. Do not upload real personal data.
  • Hosting: the app runs on Vercel and stores data in a Postgres database (Neon).
  • Nothing else is shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

  • Your test runs: use Clear my history on the Incidents page. They are deleted at once.
  • Your account record (id, email, name): email the owner at raokommanasrinivasa@gmail.com and it will be removed.
  • Signing out removes the login cookie from your browser.
